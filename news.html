Ghana–US Strengthen Defence and Security Cooperation

Ghana and the United States have reaffirmed their commitment to strengthening bilateral defence and security cooperation following a courtesy call by the US Chargé d’Affaires, HE Rolf Olson, on the Minister for Defence, Hon. Kenneth Gilbert Adjei.

Discussions covered areas including logistics, institutional development, maritime security, intelligence sharing, technology transfer, cyber defence and counter-terrorism cooperation.
