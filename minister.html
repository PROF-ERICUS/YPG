MINISTER FOR DEFENCE

Hon. Eric Andoh

Minister for Defence

Hon. Eric Andoh is an Information Technology student, Front-End Developer and Graphic Designer with a technical background in Electrical and Electronics Engineering. His academic and professional journey reflects a combination of technology, creativity, communication and practical problem-solving.

He is currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science in Information Technology at the Ghana Communication Technology University (GCTU), while building practical experience in web development, UI/UX design, graphic design and digital technology. His technical interests include HTML, CSS, JavaScript, MySQL, UI/UX design and Adobe Creative Cloud.

His professional experience includes working as a Graphic Designer at Ericsvault Hub, where he develops visual content, promotional materials and creative concepts for organisations, campaigns, events and business communications. He previously served as a Computer Teacher at Antah Academy, where he taught computer fundamentals, Microsoft Office applications, internet use and Adobe Creative Cloud tools.

Beyond his professional and academic pursuits, Hon. Eric Andoh has developed experience in student and community leadership. His leadership background includes serving as a Course Representative at GCTU, Public Relations Officer of Concerned Youth of Enmokanwo, Committee Chair of the Entrepreneurship Club at GCTU, and Vice President of GNAAS at Kikam Technical Institute.

These experiences have provided opportunities to develop communication, coordination, teamwork, public relations, organisational and problem-solving skills. His leadership experience has also involved representing students, communicating important information, supporting programmes and contributing to organisational activities.

As a technology-oriented young leader, he is interested in applying digital skills and innovative thinking to practical challenges. His work includes the development of digital solutions such as a Multi-Semester GPA & CWA Calculator and a web-based Church Attendance Management System.

His academic training, professional experience and leadership involvement provide a multidisciplinary foundation spanning information technology, engineering, digital communication, creative design and youth leadership.
