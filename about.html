WHO WE ARE

Serving through leadership, knowledge and responsibility.

The Ministry for Defence of Youth Parliament Ghana provides a platform for young people to learn, engage and participate in discussions relating to defence, national security, civic responsibility and national development.

The Ministry focuses on creating opportunities for meaningful youth participation through education, dialogue, leadership development and responsible citizenship.

Through its activities and initiatives, the Ministry encourages young people to develop an understanding of national responsibilities and contribute positively to the development of Ghana.
